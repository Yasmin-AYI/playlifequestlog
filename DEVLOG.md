# Devlog - The Life Quest Log

## 2026-11-25/26 - Day 0: The idea (Late night brainstorm)
**Decisions**
- Concept: a story-driven, game-like, halal learning app for adults
- Goal: create a new niche instead of joining an existing one
- Season 1: Cyber Detective - each case teaches one common scam
- First playable prototype of Case 01: The Fake CEO

**Why**
- Nostalgia, but still full of learning. Learning doesn't have to be boring.
- Just because you've grown up doesn't mean you have to stop playing. You can do both.
- AI is making scams smarter. Anyone can fall for a scam.

## 2026-11-26 - Day 1: Brand, mascot and setup
**Decisions** 
- Name: The Life Quest Log, handle @playlifequestlog everywhere
- Accounts: project Gmail, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, secured with 2FA
- Mascot: Mr. Figgy, a pixel owl detective (fig hat, amber eyes)
- Mr. Quest: a hidden game master behind all the scams
- Animal world: players create their own animal detective
- Story flow: the player misses the scam, Mr. Figgy steps in
- Halal by design: no music, no loot boxes, no interest
- Points: start at 100, lose points for mistakes, earn new ones daily
- Videos show the hook, the app has the full case
- Repo created: playlifequestlog (public, no license)
- Season 1 story line drafted (To be rewritten in my own words)

**Why**
- Animal characters fit with the owl concept
